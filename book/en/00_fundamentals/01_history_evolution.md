# 1.1 History and Evolution of Machine Learning

(Content translated from Spanish version - Full English version coming soon)

This chapter covers the fascinating history of AI and ML from Turing's foundational question through the recent era of Large Language Models. We discuss:

- The 1950s origins with Alan Turing and McCulloch-Pitts neurons
- The perceptron and early optimism in connectionism
- The AI winter and symbolic AI limitations
- The resurgence with statistical learning approaches
- The deep learning revolution of the 2010s
- The modern era of Transformers and LLMs

## Key Takeaways

Machine learning has gone through cycles of hype and disenchantment, but steady progress has been driven by three factors: more data, more computational power, and better algorithms.
