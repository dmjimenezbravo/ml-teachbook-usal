# 3.1 Clustering

(Content translated from Spanish version - Full English version coming soon)

This chapter covers clustering techniques for grouping similar data:

- **K-Means**: partition into K clusters, minimizing within-cluster distance
- **Elbow Method**: choosing optimal K
- **Hierarchical Clustering**: dendrograms and linkage methods
- **DBSCAN**: density-based clustering, arbitrary shapes
